# Versal Support

**Effective date:** November 11, 2026

**Developer:** Emmanuel Omokagbo

**Contact:** emmanuel.omokagbo@gmail.com

Versal is a Bible verse app. You pick how you feel, and it suggests verses to read, save, journal about and share. This page answers common questions and explains how to reach me.

## The short version

- Email **emmanuel.omokagbo@gmail.com** for help, bug reports, feedback or privacy requests.
- Versal is free. There is no subscription, no account and no sign-in.
- Your content stays on your device and in your own iCloud. I cannot see it, so I cannot recover it for you. See the [Privacy Policy](https://omokagbo.github.io/versal-policy/).

## 1. Getting started

Open Versal, choose how you feel, and it suggests a verse. You can change the Bible translation, text size and reading voice in Settings.

Versal works offline after you install it. The Bible text is stored on your device, so you do not need an internet connection to read.

## 2. Reminders

**Reminders do not arrive.**

- Check that reminders are on in Versal: Settings → Daily verse reminder.
- Check that notifications are allowed in iOS Settings → Notifications → Versal.
- Reminders are scheduled on your device and do not depend on an internet connection.

## 3. iCloud sync

Versal syncs your verses, collections, check-ins, journal entries and settings through your private iCloud, if you turn on **Sync with iCloud** in Settings.

- Make sure you are signed in to iCloud on this iPhone.
- After you change the sync setting, restart Versal. The change does not take effect until you do.
- Sync depends on Apple's iCloud service and your iCloud settings. Versal keeps working on your device while iCloud is unavailable.

If your data does not appear on a second device, wait a few minutes with both devices online, then open Versal on each one.

## 4. Your data

- **Export.** Settings → Your data → Export my data creates a JSON or text file with your journal, favorites and collections. Keep the file somewhere safe. It is the only copy outside your device and iCloud.
- **Delete.** Settings → Your data → Delete all my data removes Versal's data from your device and from your private iCloud. This cannot be undone. Export first if you want a copy.
- **Reinstalling.** If sync was off, your data lives only on this iPhone. Export before you delete the app.

## 5. Bible translations

Versal includes the Berean Standard Bible, the World English Bible and the King James Version. Versal does not offer the King James Version in the United Kingdom. In the UK, Versal offers the Berean Standard Bible and the World English Bible only.

Licenses and attributions for every text are listed in Settings → About → Credits & licenses.

If you think a verse, translation or attribution is wrong, email me and include the verse reference and the translation.

## 6. Crisis and sensitive content

Versal is for reflection and encouragement. It is not medical, psychological, counseling or crisis care.

If you are in danger or thinking about harming yourself, contact your local emergency number now. You can also contact a crisis line or a person you trust. Versal may show crisis resources, but these are not a substitute for help from a real person.

## 7. Report a problem

Email **emmanuel.omokagbo@gmail.com** with:

- A short description of what happened and what you expected.
- Your iPhone model and iOS version.
- The Versal version, if you can find it.
- A screenshot, if it helps.

Please do not include journal entries or other private content in your message unless you are comfortable sharing them.

## 8. Feedback and feature requests

Versal is a small app, and feedback shapes what I build next. Email me with ideas, verse suggestions or anything that feels off in the tone or the content. I read every message.

## 9. Privacy and legal requests

For questions about your personal data, or to ask for a copy or deletion of information I hold, email **emmanuel.omokagbo@gmail.com**. For the full terms that apply to the app, see the [Terms & Conditions](https://omokagbo.github.io/versal-terms/) and the [Privacy Policy](https://omokagbo.github.io/versal-policy/).

## 10. Contact

For anything not covered here, email **emmanuel.omokagbo@gmail.com**.
